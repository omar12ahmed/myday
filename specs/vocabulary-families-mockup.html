48 words · 12 of them already belong to a family

Arabic grows words from three-letter roots. Four roots connect 12 of your 48 starter words — and one building pattern, مَـ (ma-) “the place where…”, appears in two different families. You have been learning patterns without being told.

كِتَاب

kitāb
book

From the root ك-ت-ب, “writing”. The same root gives you “wrote”, “writes”, “books” and “library”.
